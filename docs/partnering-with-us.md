# Partnering With Us

Splitty Test is free to run for your own business, whether that's personal, internal or commercial use. The [license](/license) reserves one thing: offering Splitty Test to others as a paid or hosted service.

## When You Need a Commercial License

You need a separate written license if you want to:

* Offer Splitty Test as SaaS or a managed, hosted service
* Bundle it into a paid platform or product where it provides a substantial part of the value
* Resell or sublicense it

You **don't** need one to run Splitty Test on your own infrastructure to test your own websites and apps, even commercially.

## Get in Touch

Email [partners@splittytest.com](mailto:partners@splittytest.com) with:

* Your company and what it does
* How you'd like to offer or use Splitty Test
* The rough scale you expect (customers, traffic)

We're also happy to hear from hosting providers, agencies and integration partners who want to work with us in other ways.
